AEFH v7.5 — mathematical research programme

AEFH is an independent research programme exploring an entropy-based operator functional, stability thresholds, data-processing properties, resolvent identities, and possible physical constructions. The project distinguishes formal mathematical claims from numerical evidence and speculative physical interpretation.

Mathematical core

Operator inequalities and stability

The v7.5 manuscript brings together 14 theorem sections, interactive laboratories, expanded appendices A.4 and A.5, the finite-dimensional DPI construction, and the semifinite type II∞ proof programme.

Numerical audit

Reproducible test suite

The v7.5 master suite contains 23 numerical, symbolic, and HTML-regression checks, including SymPy and display-math guards. A separate RG suite contains 6 checks. They verify implemented code and finite-dimensional examples; they are not proofs of general statements about arbitrary von Neumann algebras.

Physical models

Constructions with explicit limits

Ten physical constructions are presented as model-dependent extensions, not automatic consequences of the mathematical core. Inputs and physical limitations remain explicit.
