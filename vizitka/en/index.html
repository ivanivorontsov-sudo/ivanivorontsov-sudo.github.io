Working principles

Reproducibility: source code, release history, and test scripts are linked where available.

Clear status: prototypes, active projects, and research hypotheses should not be presented as finished or independently verified products.

Responsible use: check each repository's README, licence, compatibility notes, and safety limitations before installing or redistributing software.
